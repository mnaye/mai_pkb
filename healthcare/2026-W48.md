# Healthcare — 2026-W48

*Week of 23 November 2026 (ISO week 48). Curated by Mai with Claude.*

## The week in one paragraph

Two regulatory stories, one finished and one undone. On **16 November** HHS's Office for Civil Rights (OCR) finalized long-pending changes to the **HIPAA Privacy Rule**: patients get copies of their records in **15 days instead of 30**, can pull them into personal health apps, and can no longer be slowed by heavy identity checks; health plans get clearer room to share data for individual care coordination. Compliance is due **17 July 2027**. Two days later, on **18 November**, the White House **pulled Heidi Overton's nomination** as FDA Commissioner a day before the Senate HELP committee vote, leaving acting commissioner Kyle Diamantas in place and resetting the 210-day clock on acting service. The agency that clears AI devices enters 2027 with no confirmed head and no nominee.

## Items

- **[HHS Finalizes HIPAA Privacy Rule Changes: What Every Healthcare Organization Needs to Know (K&L Gates, 19 November 2026)](https://www.klgates.com/thought-leadership/HHS-Finalizes-HIPAA-Privacy-Rule-Changes-What-Every-Healthcare-Organization-Needs-to-Know-11-19-2026)** `topic:enforcement`. **Agency:** HHS Office for Civil Rights. **Stage:** final rule, finalized **16 November 2026**; Federal Register publication planned for 19 November. **Effective:** 18 January 2027. **Compliance:** 17 July 2027; business associate agreements by 18 January 2028 at the latest. **Binds:** HIPAA covered entities and their business associates.
  - **Faster access:** "Cuts the deadline for acting on a request for copies of PHI from 30 to 15 calendar days," with one 30-day extension if the patient is told in writing within the first 15 days.
  - **Apps:** "Confirms that an individual may obtain electronic PHI through a personal health application" when the entity can produce it in that form; the rule also defines "electronic health record".
  - **Identity checks:** "Bars identity verification steps that impose unnecessary effort or expense."
  - **Fees:** revised permissible access fees, cases where copies must be free, and "requires advance notice of fees."
  - **Care coordination:** "Confirms that health plans may use and disclose PHI for care coordination and case management for specific individuals," and "expressly permits disclosures to social services agencies, community-based organizations, and similar third parties." 42 CFR Part 2 and stricter state consent laws still apply.
  - **Paperwork:** drops the good-faith effort to get a signed acknowledgment of the Notice of Privacy Practices; the notice must still be given and posted.
  - **Not adopted:** the 2021 proposals for a minimum-necessary exception for plan care coordination, a good-faith standard for family/caregiver disclosures, and a lower threshold for averting threats.

## So what

*For healthcare data scientists, ML engineers in healthcare, and healthcare executives.*

- **Healthcare data scientists:** The **HIPAA Privacy Rule** update lets health plans share patient data to coordinate care for one person, not just groups. It also allows sharing with social service and community groups for that care. If you build outreach or risk lists, check with privacy staff on what you can now share. Stricter state consent laws and Part 2 rules for addiction records still apply.
- **ML engineers in healthcare:** Patients must get copies of their records in **15 days**, down from 30. They can also ask for them in a personal health app. Identity checks can't add "unnecessary effort or expense." If you run a patient portal or records pipeline, time how long a request takes today. You have until **17 July 2027** to comply.
- **Healthcare executives:** Two things to plan for. First, the **HIPAA** rule has a firm compliance date of **17 July 2027**. Budget now for faster records requests, new fee notices and updated vendor contracts (business associate agreements). Second, the White House **withdrew Heidi Overton** as FDA Commissioner on 18 November. The acting head is reported to leave at year's end, and there is no new nominee. Expect slower or shifting FDA policy on AI devices. Don't plan launches around a new FDA chief's agenda.

### Problems worth taking on

**A 15-day records-request clock.** *Problem:* Patients wait weeks for their records, and staff fill requests by hand. Missing the deadline is a HIPAA risk. *Why now:* The **HIPAA Privacy Rule** final rule cuts the deadline from 30 to 15 days, with compliance by 17 July 2027. *First step:* Have the health-information office pull last quarter's access requests. Measure the days from request to delivery, and where each one stalls. Success is a list of the three slowest steps and a fix for each, with most requests under 15 days.
