# Healthcare — 2026-W35

*Week of 24–30 August 2026. Curated by Mai with Claude.*

## The week in one paragraph

The week's dominant story is a **Phase 3 win for an individualized mRNA cancer therapy**: on 19 August, Merck and Moderna reported that INTerpath-001 met both its primary endpoint (recurrence-free survival) and its key secondary endpoint (distant metastasis-free survival) in completely resected stage IIB–IV melanoma. It is the first positive Phase 3 readout for a patient-specific neoantigen therapy and the first Phase 3 to beat KEYTRUDA alone in the adjuvant melanoma setting — validating a manufacturing model where each dose is built from an individual patient's tumor sequencing, which is a supply-chain problem as much as a biology one. Underneath that headline, health IT kept consolidating rather than raising: the week's deal flow was almost entirely acquisitions (Hinge Health, iRhythm, Kyndryl, Providence Equity) with only small growth rounds attached. Regulatory attention this month sat on **digitally-derived endpoints** — how sensor and wearable data become evidence a regulator will accept.

## Items

- **Merck + Moderna: intismeran autogene meets Phase 3 endpoints in resected melanoma** *(research)* — INTerpath-001 randomized **1,137 patients** with completely resected stage IIB–IV cutaneous melanoma **2:1** to intismeran autogene (V940 / mRNA-4157) plus KEYTRUDA versus KEYTRUDA alone (NCT05933577). Both **RFS** (primary) and **DMFS** (key secondary) showed "statistically significant and clinically meaningful improvements"; **hazard ratios were not disclosed in the release**. Safety was "consistent with those observed in previously reported studies for the combination, with no new safety signals observed." The companies plan to present at an upcoming international medical meeting and to "engage with regulators on filing submissions." For scale, the earlier Phase 2b KEYNOTE-942 data cited in the release showed a 49% reduction in risk of recurrence or death (HR=0.51; 95% CI 0.294–0.887) and 59% for distant metastasis or death (HR=0.411; 95% CI 0.200–0.843) — Phase 2b numbers, not Phase 3. **Released 2026-08-19.** [Merck press release](https://www.merck.com/news/merck-and-moderna-announce-phase-3-interpath-001-trial-of-intismeran-autogene-plus-keytruda-met-endpoints-of-recurrence-free-survival-rfs-and-distant-metastasis-free-survival-dmfs-in-patient/) · coverage: [STAT](https://www.statnews.com/2026/08/19/mrna-cancer-vaccine-trial-melanoma-merck-moderna/), [Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/merck-and-modernas-personalized-cancer-vaccine-slows-recurrence-ph-3-trial)

- **Health IT is buying, not funding** *(market)* — The week's financial ledger is lopsided toward M&A: **Hinge Health acquired Cylinder Health for $105M cash**, **iRhythm acquired VitalConnect**, **Kyndryl acquired Healthcare IT Leaders**, **Providence Equity took a majority stake in CheckedUp (18 Aug)**, and **EnableComp acquired Helix Advisory** — against a single sizeable round, **HealthSnap's $25M senior secured growth financing** led by Eastward Capital Partners. Debt-flavored growth capital plus strategic roll-ups is what a sector looks like when it is consolidating rather than expanding. *Source: [Health IT Answers, Financial Edition, 2026-08-20](https://www.healthitanswers.net/health-it-business-news-financial-edition-august-20-2026/) — a secondary aggregator; verify individual deal terms at the company before citing.*

## Also noted

- **FDA public workshop on statistical considerations for digitally-derived endpoints** — scheduled **27 August 2026**, virtual and free, with updates on FDA's PDUFA VII digital-health-technology work. The regulatory question that gates most remote-monitoring and wearable products is not the sensor but whether its output is an endpoint a reviewer will accept. Announced via FDA's digital health technologies program: [FDA — DHTs for Drug Development](https://www.fda.gov/science-research/science-and-research-special-topics/digital-health-technologies-dhts-drug-development). *Date sourced from search results, not from a fetched FDA page — confirm before relying on it.*
- **FDA's DHT funding line.** A U01 cooperative agreement with a **$1.1M award ceiling** and two expected awards, for sponsors using DHTs (actigraphy, photography, contactless sensors) in trials supporting drug development; applications closed **20 August 2026**. Secondary source: [FDA Law Blog](https://www.thefdalawblog.com/2026/08/a-sensor-for-every-symptom-fda-puts-1-1-million-behind-digital-health-in-drug-trials/).
- **AI device base rate, for context.** The FDA has now authorized **1,000+ AI/ML-enabled medical devices**, roughly 95–97% via 510(k), with radiology over 75% of clearances. Useful denominator when a single new clearance gets framed as a milestone. Figures are aggregator-compiled — treat as approximate, and check the [FDA AI-enabled device list](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/software-medical-device-samd/artificial-intelligence-enabled-medical-devices) directly for anything citable.
