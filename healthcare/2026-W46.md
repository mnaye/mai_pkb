# Healthcare — 2026-W46

*Week of 9 November 2026 (ISO week 46). Curated by Mai with Claude.*

## The week in one paragraph

Medicare's two big annual payment rules landed on **4 November**. The **CY 2027 Physician Fee Schedule final rule** cuts the main payment rate by **2.31%** for most clinicians, which is deeper than the July proposal. It also gives practices two more years, to **1 January 2029**, before Medicare pays for remote patient monitoring (RPM) and remote therapeutic monitoring (RTM) only when the practice's own clinical staff do the work. Alongside it, the **CY 2027 OPPS final rule** raises hospital outpatient rates **2.3%**, finalizes site-neutral pay for imaging without contrast at some off-campus departments, and moves toward ending the inpatient-only list by 2029. Both rules take effect in January 2027. There was no news this run on FDA's generative-AI device discussion paper or on the FDA Commissioner nomination.

## Items

- **[CY 2027 Medicare Physician Fee Schedule final rule (CMS fact sheet)](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/calendar-year-cy-2027-medicare-physician-fee-schedule-final-rule)** `topic:payment`. **Agency:** CMS. **Stage:** final rule (CMS-1848-F), fact sheet dated **4 November 2026**. **Effective:** services "on or after Jan. 1, 2027." **Binds:** clinicians and practices billing Medicare Part B.
  - **Pay rate:** the non-APM conversion factor is **$32.63**, a projected 2.31% cut. "The CY 2027 qualifying APM conversion factor of $32.95 represents a projected decrease of $0.62 (-1.83%)." [Fierce Healthcare](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/regulatory/cms-finalizes-final-medicare-rule-deeper-cuts-physician-pay-delays-rpm-restrictions-2029) (4 November) gives the 2026 rate as $33.40 and says CMS "finalized steeper physician payment cuts than it had proposed in July" (1.68% non-APM was proposed).
  - **Remote monitoring:** CMS is "delaying for two years, to Jan. 1, 2029, the requirement to only allow payment for RPM or RTM services" furnished by the practice's own clinical staff. Per Fierce, from 2027 RTM is limited to established patients, and an initiating visit is required before billing RPM or RTM.
  - **ACOs:** a voluntary modifier for Shared Savings Program ACO practitioners would "increase payment of the associated E/M visit by 32%." Per Fierce, the BASIC Level E shared savings rate rises "from 50% to 60%."
  - **Same-day visits:** per Fierce, the modifier 25 cut is 20%, down from the proposed 50%.
  - **Telehealth:** the fact sheet notes CAA 2026 extended RHC/FQHC payment for non-behavioral visits by telecommunication "through Dec. 31, 2027."
  - **Reaction:** MGMA said it "is disappointed by CMS's decision to finalize several destabilizing policies" (via Fierce).

## So what

*For healthcare data scientists, ML engineers in healthcare, and healthcare executives.*

- **Healthcare data scientists:** The **Physician Fee Schedule** rule cuts Medicare's base pay rate by 2.31% for most doctors in 2027. That is deeper than CMS proposed in July. Re-run any 2027 revenue forecast that used the July number. The **OPPS** rule adds 2.3% for hospital outpatient care. Model the two together if your system has both clinics and hospitals.
- **ML engineers in healthcare:** If you build remote patient monitoring (RPM) tools, you have more time. CMS delayed the rule that only a practice's own staff can do paid RPM or RTM work until 1 January 2029. Outside vendors can keep doing that work until then. Use the time to make sure your tools work for in-house staff too. From 2027, Fierce reports a visit is needed before RPM or RTM billing starts. Build that check into your enrollment flow.
- **Healthcare executives:** Plan for 2.31% less per unit of Medicare doctor pay starting 1 January 2027. If you lean on an outside RPM vendor, you now have until 2029 to bring that work in-house or change the deal. ACO doctors can get 32% more for a visit through a new, optional billing code. Ask finance whether that changes the case for joining or staying in an ACO. Hospital leaders should check which off-campus imaging sites lose pay under the new site-neutral rule.

### Problems worth taking on

**Plan the RPM move to in-house staff before 2029.** *Problem:* Many practices pay outside vendors to run remote monitoring. Under the final rule, Medicare will stop paying for vendor-done RPM and RTM work in 2029. *Why now:* The **Physician Fee Schedule** final rule (4 November) set the 1 January 2029 date. *First step:* Have the RPM program lead list every patient and task now done by a vendor. Have finance price the staff needed to do it in-house. Success is a cost plan for 2029 ready by mid-2027.
