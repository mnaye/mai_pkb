# Healthcare — 2026-W40

*Week of 28 September 2026 (ISO week 40). Curated by Mai with Claude.*

## The week in one paragraph

A quiet week at the Federal Register — six HHS rules and proposed rules in seven days, none of them a payment bombshell — but one of them rewrites language that sits underneath every IND and NDA in the country. On **22 September** FDA issued a **direct final rule on "Nonclinical Testing Terminology"**, striking the word *animal* out of five parts of 21 CFR and replacing it with *nonclinical*, so that the regulations stop presuming animal studies are the way safety gets demonstrated. It is deliberately a low-drama vehicle — a direct final rule with a companion proposed rule, withdrawn automatically if anyone files a significant adverse comment — and that is the point: the agency is treating "New Approach Methodologies are acceptable evidence" as uncontroversial housekeeping rather than a fight. Paired with FDA's **21 September** press announcement on advancing alternatives to animal testing, this is the week the shift stopped being guidance-level encouragement and became codified text. Two smaller items also landed: a **CMS RFI on Medicare Part D pharmacy contracting standards** and an **HHS interim final rule** codifying temporary moratoria on ACA Exchange agent/broker registration.

## Items

- **[FDA — Nonclinical Testing Terminology (direct final rule)](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/09/22/2026-19350/nonclinical-testing-terminology)** `topic:fda` — **Agency:** FDA. **Stage:** direct final rule, published **22 September 2026**, with a [companion proposed rule](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/09/22/2026-19349/nonclinical-testing-terminology) issued the same day. **Docket FDA-2026-N-5347, RIN 0910-AJ27.** **Effective 4 February 2027; comments close 7 December 2026.** **Amends 21 CFR Parts 312, 314, 315, 361 and 601** — i.e. INDs, NDAs/ANDAs, diagnostic radiopharmaceuticals, radioactive drugs for research, and biologics licensing. The change replaces terminology that emphasizes **animal** testing with **nonclinical** language across those safety regulations and adds definitions, with FDA's stated aim to "foster the development and use of scientifically valid new testing methodologies" while holding the substantive safety standard constant. **Who it binds:** every drug and biologic sponsor filing under those parts — the requirement is still to show adequate nonclinical safety evidence, but the regulation no longer reads as though only animal data can supply it. **Watch the mechanism:** because it is a *direct* final rule, FDA will publish a document confirming the effective date within 30 days of the comment close if **no significant adverse comment** arrives — and will **withdraw the rule entirely**, within the same 30 days, if one does. So the companion proposed rule is the fallback path, and 7 December is the real date to diary. Context on the same theme: FDA's **21 September** press announcement, ["FDA Updates Regulations to Advance Innovative Alternatives to Animal Testing"](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/fda-newsroom/press-announcements). *Rule metadata retrieved 2026-09-28 from the Federal Register API; dates and CFR parts quoted from that record.*

## Also noted

From the same Federal Register sweep of HHS rules and proposed rules published **21–28 September 2026** (fetched 2026-09-28) — titles and abstracts as the Register reports them, not separately fetched:

- **[CMS — Request for Information: Medicare Part D Reasonable and Relevant Pharmacy Contracting Standards](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/09/24/2026-19535/request-for-information-medicare-part-d-reasonable-and-relevant-pharmacy-contracting-standards)** (**24 Sep**, proposed rule / RFI) — CMS is gathering input on standards for "reasonable and relevant" pharmacy contract terms under Part D, implementing a requirement from the **Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2026**. Pre-rulemaking, but it is the opening move on PBM–pharmacy contract terms in Part D and worth tracking for whoever eventually has to comply.
- **[HHS — ACA; Temporary Moratoria on Certain Agent and Broker Registration](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/09/23/2026-19493/patient-protection-and-affordable-care-act-temporary-moratoria-on-certain-agent-and-broker)** (**23 Sep**, interim final rule) — codifies HHS authority to pause registration of agents and brokers lacking current Exchange registrations, **effective through 1 February 2027**. A program-integrity response to unauthorized-enrollment problems.
- **FDA food-additive actions (25 Sep):** a [final rule permitting vitamin D2 inactive bakers yeast](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/09/25/2026-19656/food-additives-permitted-for-direct-addition-to-food-for-human-consumption-vitamin-d2-inactive) as a vitamin D source in specified food categories (Lallemand petition), and [filing of a Doehler color-additive petition for calcium sulfate](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/09/25/2026-19658/filing-of-color-additive-petition-from-doehler-gmbh-request-to-amend-the-color-additive-regulations). Routine; listed for completeness of the week's docket.
