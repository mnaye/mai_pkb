# Research Skill — Healthcare Trends

A focused source guide for the `healthcare/` area. The weekly brief loads this when researching healthcare industry trends.

## Goal

Surface the most notable **new** healthcare developments from the past 7 days — industry moves, research, and market movement. Cover:

- **Digital health & health tech** — AI in clinical care, diagnostics, remote monitoring, EHR, telehealth.
- **Industry & market** — funding rounds, M&A, product launches, provider/payer/pharma moves, policy and regulation (FDA approvals, reimbursement).
- **Research** — clinical and translational studies with real-world impact.

## How to research

1. **Web-search first** for recent stories (past 7 days), pairing a theme with the sector, e.g.:
   - `digital health funding 2026`, `FDA clearance AI diagnostic`
   - `healthcare AI clinical trial results`, `health tech acquisition`
2. **Fetch (scrape) the actual article pages** from the priority sources below for real titles, summaries, and links.

**Regulatory**
- FDA news & approvals — https://www.fda.gov/news-events

## What to capture per item

- **Name / headline** (bold)
- One-line description — what it is and why it matters
- **Link** to the fetched source
- Category tag where useful (funding / regulation / research / product)

## Guardrails

- **Only include articles you actually fetched** — no invented links, figures, or quotes.
- Prefer primary sources (the journal, the company/FDA announcement) over secondary coverage.
- Be precise with numbers (funding amounts, trial results) — quote them only from the fetched source.
- Attribute curation to **Mai with Claude**.
