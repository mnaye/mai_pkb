## The week in one paragraph

The frontier-model release and the grassroots trending list pulled in opposite directions this week, and the gap between them is the story. Anthropic shipped **Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1** on 1 September — a frontier coding-and-knowledge-work release, cloud-hosted, with a same-day enterprise-safeguards post alongside it. Meanwhile the single fastest-rising new repo on weekly trending, [`magnitudedev/magnitude`](https://github.com/magnitudedev/magnitude) (**+1,961 stars**), exists to do the reverse: profile your hardware, pick a local model that fits it, and plug that into the coding agent you already use. Last week's "skills as the unit of distribution" thesis also held — [`tt-a1i/archify`](https://github.com/tt-a1i/archify) is now at **52k stars** after a second consecutive ~17k week. Track 2 (productionizing healthcare models) again produced nothing datable to this window.

## Items

- **[Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Mythos 5.1](https://www.anthropic.com/news)** — Anthropic's new frontier models for coding and knowledge work, announced **1 September 2026**. Released the same day as a companion post, *"Developing Enterprise Frontier Safeguards with our customers"*, which is the more interesting pairing: the capability release and the deployment-controls release are now shipping together rather than the safeguards trailing by a quarter. Model IDs follow the existing `claude-fable-5-1` convention, so migration is a string change for anyone already on the 5-family. *Verified against [anthropic.com/news](https://www.anthropic.com/news), fetched 2026-09-07. Linked to the news index rather than a guessed post slug.*

- **[magnitudedev/magnitude](https://github.com/magnitudedev/magnitude)** — "Open source inference server that runs the best local models for your hardware, plugged into the agent you already use." It profiles the machine, recommends models sized to it with estimated tokens/sec, then downloads and configures them; adds just-in-time model loading, automatic unloading under memory pressure, and fully offline operation once weights are local. Integrates with Pi, Cline, and Claude Code, or runs standalone. **3.9k stars, +1,961 this week** — the largest relative jump on the weekly list (roughly a 100% gain in seven days), and 618 commits. The notable design choice is that setup is *agent-guided*: your coding assistant walks you through installing the thing that will serve its own local alternative. *Fetched 2026-09-07 from the repo page.*

- **The skill-bundle wave did not crest.** [`tt-a1i/archify`](https://github.com/tt-a1i/archify) — W36's breakout, at 37.7k stars then — is now **52,064 stars (+17,190 this week)**, a second consecutive week of roughly the same enormous gain rather than the usual one-week spike and decay. [`THU-MAIC/OpenMAIC`](https://github.com/THU-MAIC/OpenMAIC) tracked the same pattern, **32,875 (+9,193)**, up from 26.4k/+2,085 in W36 — its weekly gain more than quadrupled. Two repos compounding for a third week is past the point where it reads as trending-page noise; the packaged, harness-loadable skill is holding as a distribution unit. *Source: [GitHub Trending, weekly](https://github.com/trending?since=weekly), fetched 2026-09-07.*
