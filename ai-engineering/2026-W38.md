# AI Engineering — 2026-W38

*Week of 14 September 2026 (ISO week 38). Curated by Mai with Claude.*

## The week in one paragraph

After three weeks of frontier releases, the interesting move this week came from the cheap end and the plumbing. **DeepSeek shipped V4.1-Flash on 10 September** under MIT — a 552B mixture-of-experts model on a new causal encoder-decoder architecture that the company claims beats its own much larger V4-Pro, and which from **14 September silently absorbs V4-Pro API traffic at Flash prices**. That is a vendor retiring its own flagship into its small model, which is a different kind of news than a capability bump. Alongside it, **OpenAI now maintains an official plugin catalog for Codex** with a declared manifest format (`.codex-plugin/plugin.json`) — the packaged-capability unit this knowledge base has tracked since W36 now has a first-party registry rather than only community skill repos. The community side did not slow: GitHub's weekly trending list is still almost entirely agent-skill bundles. Track 2 (productionizing healthcare models) produced nothing datable to this window for the **third consecutive week**.

## Items

- **[DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/10/deepseek-releases-v4-1-flash-says-it-outperforms-flagship-v4-pro/)** — Released **10 September 2026** under an **MIT license** with open weights. A mixture-of-experts model with **552B total parameters** (roughly double V4-Flash's 284B) on a new *Causal Encoder-Decoder* architecture, with **8B active per token on input and 16B on output**, a **1M context window**, and native image understanding. DeepSeek says third-party tests put it ahead of the much larger **V4-Pro** on performance, cost, speed and total runtime. The operational detail is the sharper one: **from 14 September, API calls to V4-Pro are answered by V4.1-Flash and billed at the smaller model's rate** until a V4.1-Pro ships, and both V4-Flash and August's experimental vision model are retired onto it. For anyone with DeepSeek in a production path, this is a silent model swap under an unchanged endpoint — worth pinning versions before the 14th. *Reported by SiliconANGLE, 10 Sep 2026; see also the [DeepSeek API change log](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/updates/). **Not fetched from the primary source** — this run's fetch budget was spent; figures are as SiliconANGLE reported them.*

- **[openai/plugins](https://github.com/openai/plugins)** — OpenAI's own "curated collection of Codex plugin examples," **6.7k stars (+1,181 this week)** and **306 commits**. Plugins live under `plugins/<name>/` and are declared by a required **`.codex-plugin/plugin.json` manifest**; the shipped examples cover Figma, Notion, iOS/macOS app development, web apps, Expo, Netlify, Remotion and Google Slides. Read next to **[openai/skills](https://github.com/openai/skills)** ("Skills Catalog for Codex", **27,158 ★, +1,622**), the shape of the thing is clear: the packaged, harness-loadable capability has gone from a community convention to a vendor-maintained format with a first-party registry. That is the same unit the archify/superpowers wave has been distributing for a month, now with a manifest schema attached. *Repo page fetched 2026-09-14; star deltas from the weekly trending fetch, same date.*

- **The trending page is now, essentially, a skills marketplace.** Of the 23 repos on [GitHub Trending (weekly)](https://github.com/trending?since=weekly) this week, the majority are agent-skill bundles rather than libraries or runtimes. The **#1 entry is [`ayghri/i-have-adhd`](https://github.com/ayghri/i-have-adhd)** — "a skill to stop your coding agent from burying the answer" — at **45,138 ★, +16,740**, the largest weekly gain on the board, and it is a prompt-shaping skill, not code. [`tt-a1i/archify`](https://github.com/tt-a1i/archify) reached **61,827 ★ (+10,132)**, its *third* consecutive ~10k+ week; [`cathrynlavery/diagram-design`](https://github.com/cathrynlavery/diagram-design) (38 editorial diagram types, "No Mermaid slop") is at **39,645 ★ (+7,129)**. The competitive axis in agentic coding this month is not model quality or harness architecture — it is whose skill bundle a developer installs. *Source: weekly trending page, fetched 2026-09-14.*
